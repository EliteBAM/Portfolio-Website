When my DM  first reached the Order of BitShift, I was obliged by a BitKeeper to answer three questions-- By tradition, I shall do so now...

❖1   What do you enjoy most about game development?

A tough question indeed.

In the abstract, it is the process of turning a high-level concept born of pure imagination into something concrete, quantified, and real. Something shareable that uncovers people who resonate with your imagination. That's what I enjoy most about game development. Further, I love the iterative process of ideating about the "fun" in systems, distilling them, and discovering the gaps in one's human intuition through the process of implementation and taking away the insights gained therein.

In practice, I enjoy many aspects of game development. As I said, I quite enjoy the process of architecting something qualitative and informal into something procedural. I love coding systems, whether they be gameplay systems, backend systems, or any system that blurs the line between the two. That being said, I also enjoy and am experienced in design and art as well as systems and code. I have a knack for pixel art, and art in general. I am experienced in Maya, ZBrush, Substance Painter, and the like.

I'd like to think I have a good sense for design and understanding the abstract "essence" of what ideas are within a game's intended identity and what ideas stray from it, and I'm ready and willing to use that insight coupled with my broad skillset in an agile manner where needed!

❖2   What would you like to get out of working at Bitshift?

My application to Bitshift is not one of many-- in case it wasn't clear, I basically went all in. That's because Bitshift is making a game that I'm truly compelled by, in an environment that I'm compelled to be a part of. Raytracing sparse voxel octrees? Yeah, count me in.

In working at Bitshift, and in my life as principle, I'm looking to follow my passion and create something awesome. I don't just want to work at any studio on any big name title. I want to work at Bitshift for all the things that make Bitshift Bishift. If my application doesn't work out, I suppose I could order white face paint, a red nose, big shoes, read a book on addiction psychology, and reluctantly apply to the AAA gamebling industry.

In addition to working on something I truly care about, I want to collaborate in a tight-knit manner with like-minded people-- meet lifelong collaborators and friends. I want to contribute to an atmosphere of open discourse and dense development. I want to be part of a team with harcore motivation, high skill and adaptability, and passion for the craft. Finally, I want to make something that I truly consider one of my life's works.

I want to put in the work with an inspired and up-beat team and get an iconic, fun game out of it!
