The magnitudes of these forces is calculated using the lift or drag formula, and the direction of the force is either parallel to the fluid velocity (drag) or perpendicular to it (lift).

The magnitudes vary directly by the area of the airfoil and the coefficient of lift or drag, which is a function of angle of attack.

The magnitudes also vary directly by the properties of the fluid they travel in. The sail varies by the density and velocity of the apparent wind, while the centerboard forces are affected by different values set for the ocean's density and realtime apparent velocity.
