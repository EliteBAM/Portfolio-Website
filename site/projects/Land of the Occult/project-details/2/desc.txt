Land of the Occult is a little dungeon crawler roguelike I built in Unity. It has great vibes and I find it very fun and challenging, even though the code is a bit wonky! I'm adding this to my portfolio for the design and atmosphere check, as well as some pixel art.

I designed the game and was the only one working in-engine.

I made all the pixel art and animations for the main character and coded the game.

I had some team members make the art for the environment and the art for the cult enemies.

The end credits list way more people than who actually worked on it, as it was a school project with many "non-contributors." It was just me and two other artists as mentioned above.
